<CaseStudy.Context>

A CI/CD pipeline is usually a sequence of scripts: when a stage fails, someone reads the log, works out what happened and decides what to do. This personal project explores one question: **what changes when an AI agent drives each stage**, from build to deployment on Kubernetes?

The idea connects two things I am studying now: the postgraduate program in DevOps and Cloud Architecture, and using AI to take repetitive work out of the way.

</CaseStudy.Context>
