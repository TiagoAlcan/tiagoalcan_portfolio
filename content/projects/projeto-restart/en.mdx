<CaseStudy.Context>

People who lose a pet and people who find one post in different places, and the two posts rarely meet. projeto_restart is an end-to-end data project to bring those two ends together: collect the posts, organize them and show the result in an interactive dashboard.

</CaseStudy.Context>

<CaseStudy.Process>

<CaseStudy.Step title="Design before building">
The repository starts with the architecture. Six diagrams describe the system from different angles: overview, components, data flow, deployment, sequence and states.
</CaseStudy.Step>

<CaseStudy.Step title="Data layers without heavy infrastructure">
Data moves through three layers, <Mark>raw, cleaned and ready to use</Mark>, simulated locally as folders. The idea is to get the discipline of a data lake without needing the cloud on day one.
</CaseStudy.Step>

<Terminal title="project_restart/" lines={["src/api/          backend", "src/scrapers/     data collection", "src/ml_engine/    inference and matching", "src/dashboard/    frontend", "data/raw  →  data/processed  →  data/curated", "", "$ docker compose up"]} />

<CaseStudy.Step title="One command to bring everything up">
Instead of several repositories or Kubernetes from the start, a single Docker Compose file brings up the whole development environment.
</CaseStudy.Step>

</CaseStudy.Process>

<CaseStudy.Result>

The project is in progress. Today the public repository holds the architecture and the diagrams; the implementation is not published yet.

</CaseStudy.Result>
